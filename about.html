About Odoo Partners Directory

Why this site exists, what it is (and isn't), and how we keep the data honest.

What this site is

Odoo Partners Directory is an independent, searchable catalog of 464 official Odoo implementation partner companies across 16 countries, organized by country and certification tier (Gold, Silver, Ready). Alongside the directory, we publish original guides on Odoo implementation cost, module overviews, and how Odoo compares to alternatives like SAP, Zoho, and QuickBooks — written to answer the questions people actually search for before starting an ERP project.

Why we built it

Finding the right Odoo implementation partner is hard: Odoo's own partner directory is thorough but not easy to filter or compare at a glance, and most "best Odoo partner" content online is thin or written by a partner promoting themselves. We built this as a neutral, searchable reference point — no listing is paid for or ranked by anything other than the tier Odoo itself assigns.

Where the data comes from

Every company listing — including any phone number, email, website, and address shown — is pulled directly from that company's own public profile page on Odoo's official partner directory. Nothing is invented or estimated. Where a profile doesn't publish a phone or email, the card says so and links to the official profile instead. Listings are refreshed periodically; the authoritative, most current source is always odoo.com/partners.

What this site is not

This is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Odoo S.A. "Odoo" is a trademark of Odoo S.A. We don't sell placement, accept payment for listing order, or charge partner companies to appear — every company on Odoo's public directory in our covered countries is eligible to be listed, free.
