What do Gold, Silver, and Ready mean?

Odoo ranks its implementation partners into three tiers based on project history, certified consultants, and sales volume — not on price or quality of service, which still varies partner to partner.

🥇 Gold Partner

The most experienced tier — typically dozens to hundreds of completed implementations and multiple Odoo-certified consultants on staff. Best fit for larger or more complex rollouts (multi-company, multi-country, heavy customization).

🥈 Silver Partner

Established firms with a solid project track record, often specializing in a particular industry or region. A strong middle-ground choice for most small-to-mid-sized implementations.

🔰 Ready Partner

Newer to the Odoo partner program or smaller/boutique firms. Can still be excellent for straightforward, standard-module implementations, often at a lower cost — worth asking for references regardless of tier.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the right Odoo partner?

Start by matching tier to project complexity (see above), then narrow by country/time-zone and industry experience. Always ask a shortlisted partner for 2-3 references from businesses similar in size and industry to yours, and get a fixed-scope quote for phase one rather than an open-ended hourly estimate.

Is this an official Odoo website?

No. This is an independent, unofficial directory. All listings are sourced from Odoo's own public partner directory at odoo.com/partners and link out to each company's real, official Odoo profile page for verified details. Why isn't my country or company listed?

This directory currently covers a curated set of 16 countries and is refreshed periodically, not a live mirror of Odoo's full partner list. The authoritative, complete, and most current list is always odoo.com/partners.

Does Odoo partner tier affect pricing?

Not directly — tier reflects experience and scale, not a fixed rate card. Gold partners are often (not always) priced higher given demand and seniority, but a well-matched Silver or Ready partner can be more cost-effective for a straightforward implementation.
