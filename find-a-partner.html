How this works

This tool scores every partner with published project data on three signals pulled straight from that company's own official Odoo profile: how many of their past projects were in your industry, whether their typical project size matches yours, and their client retention rate. It's a starting shortlist, not a guarantee — always verify fit with a direct conversation and references before signing anything. See the full partner evaluation checklist for what to ask next.

About this data: Match scores are computed from real per-partner statistics (project counts by industry, typical project size, customer retention) published on each company's own official Odoo partner profile — nothing is invented.
